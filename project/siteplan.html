Course Assignment: WDD 131 - Project Site Plan

Site Name

Proposed Name: Tri-City Hidden Gems

Rationale: The name "Tri-City Hidden Gems" fits our branding perfectly by highlighting a focus on lesser-known, high-value local spots rather than mainstream tourist attractions. It immediately communicates to our target audience of adventurous locals and curious travelers that they will discover unique community experiences. Furthermore, it explicitly aligns with our project goal of creating a structured, multi-city regional directory.

Target Domain Name: tricityhiddengems.org

Site Purpose

The scope of Tri-City Hidden Gems is to serve as an accessible, community-driven digital guidebook for the local regional area. The target audience includes residents seeking weekend activities, food enthusiasts looking for off-the-beaten-path dining, and domestic travelers wanting authentic cultural experiences. The core goal of the system is to organize local attractions into clean, filterable categories. The website delivers real value by providing verified physical coordinates, crowd levels, dynamic mapping data, seasonal travel checklists, and a direct form where users can submit their own local recommendations.

Target Audience Scenarios

Potential users visiting this website configuration seek quick answers to the following specific inquiries:

  • Scenario 1: Where can I find a quiet, scenic hiking trail or park within the tri-city area that is dog-friendly and has free parking?
  • Scenario 2: What are the top-rated local, family-owned coffee shops or restaurants open after 8:00 PM on weekdays?
